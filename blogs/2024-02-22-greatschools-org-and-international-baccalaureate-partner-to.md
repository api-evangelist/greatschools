GreatSchools.org partners with International Baccalaureate to connect K-12 families with IB World Schools and their advanced academic offerings. This new data partnership aims to help parents find high-quality education options for their children, expanding GreatSchools' efforts to raise awareness of rigorous academic programs. The collaboration seeks to empower families to discover transformative opportunities offered by IB World Schools.
