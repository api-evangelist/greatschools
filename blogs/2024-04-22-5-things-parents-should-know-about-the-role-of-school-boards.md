School boards hold significant influence on students' education, overseeing spending and policies. They set the district's educational vision, choose curriculums and bus routes, hire superintendents, and engage with the community. Amid nonpartisan elections, parents can empower themselves by understanding these roles and engaging with the board to ensure their children receive quality education.
