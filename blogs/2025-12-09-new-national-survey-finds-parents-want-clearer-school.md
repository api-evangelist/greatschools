Oakland, CA (December 9, 2025) — A new nationwide survey from GreatSchools.org reveals that parents want clearer, more complete information about their schools — along with communication that is easy to access and understand. The survey of more than 1,000 K–12 parents showed a strong correlation between parents’ overall satisfaction with their school (and willingness to recommend it to others) and their experience finding important information.
